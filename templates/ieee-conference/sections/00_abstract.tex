\begin{abstract}
Writing academic papers across diverse platforms (tablets, mobile devices, and desktop workstations) often introduces version divergence and toolchain fragmentation. In this paper, we propose a lightweight, serverless framework that unifies collaborative LaTeX authoring, automated cloud compilation, and bidirectional knowledge base synthesis. By decoupling modular sections and leveraging asynchronous version control alongside multi-agent reasoning, our approach eliminates vendor lock-in while preserving real-time author ergonomics. Experimental evaluation on active research workflows demonstrates significant reductions in compilation latency and collaboration overhead.
\end{abstract}
